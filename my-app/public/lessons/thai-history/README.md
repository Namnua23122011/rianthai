# Thai history slideshow

The 16 numbered images are rendered, in their original order, from the
user-provided `ความเป็นมาของภาษาไทย.pdf` on 2026-11-22.

The original PDF is unchanged. All source pages, including review and answer
slides, are retained. Poppler rendered the pages at 1920 × 1080 as progressive
JPEGs (quality 90), totaling approximately 5.9 MB instead of the 81.6 MB PDF.

Page labels and image paths are in `src/thaiHistorySlides.js`. The interactive
viewer is `src/SlideLesson.jsx`.
